# Scenario

A hospital is reviewing staffing levels in its Emergency Department (ED).

Recently:

* Waiting times have increased
* Staff feel under pressure
* Managers are concerned about costs

Patients arrive throughout the day needing treatment.
Some cases are quick to resolve, while others require more time and attention.

Doctors are shared across all incoming patients.

Hospital management want to understand:

> How should we staff the department so that patients are treated efficiently while maintaining quality of care?

They have asked you to investigate and propose an approach.
