# Sample questions 2023/24 (older format)

**Questions:**
1. linear programming;
2. simulation;
3. transportation;
4. inventory (EOQ, not a current focus);
5. moving-average forecasting.

These are good for extra method practice.

## Steps

1. Attempt **`sample-questions-2023-24.pdf`**.
2. Mark it against **`sample-questions-2023-24-answers.pdf`**.
3. Follow the Excel working in `sample-questions-2023-24-answers-workings.xlsx` (Q2, Q4, Q5).
4. Run **`sample_questions_2023_24_answers.ipynb`** on the
   [course website](https://invariantum.github.io/operational_research/) or on your computer. It has the
   Pyomo answers to Q1 and Q3.
