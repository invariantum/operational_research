# Templates

**`pyomo_starter_template.ipynb`** is the smallest complete Pyomo model (two variables, two constraints)
solved with HiGHS. Use it as the starting point for your own LP/MILP models in Coursework Part A, for
revision or in the test.

1. Open it on the
   [course website](https://invariantum.github.io/operational_research/lab/index.html?path=Assignments/5cm522-operational-research-public/03-revision/templates/pyomo_starter_template.ipynb)
   or on your computer.
2. Run the first cell (installs Pyomo and HiGHS when needed), then the model cell.
3. Change the variables, objective and constraints to your problem. Download your copy to keep it.
