# 5CM522 Timed In-Class Test

## Open-Book Materials Guidance

Recommended position for the May 2026 in-class test:

- Students may use lecture slides.
- Students may use tutorial sheets and tutorial solutions discussed in class.
- Students may use their own notes.
- Students may use their own code files and generic class example code.
- Students may use module materials released through normal teaching.
- Students must not use AI tools.

Recommended restriction:

- Release the practice papers and their written solutions before the test.
- Do not release the coded solutions for `Practice Paper A` or `Practice Paper B` before the test.
- Do not release any coded solution pack for the live in-class test before the test.

## Rationale

This keeps the assessment genuinely open book without turning it into a near-copying exercise.

Students should be able to:

- refer to methods
- reuse general modelling patterns
- adapt lecture and tutorial examples
- use code structures they have developed themselves

However, they should still need to:

- identify the correct OR method from the scenario
- map the scenario into variables, constraints and objective functions
- adapt code rather than simply change a few numbers
- interpret results in context

## Why This Boundary Is Fair

In an operational research test, some template reuse is normal and desirable.
Well-prepared students should benefit from having organised notes, worked examples and reusable code patterns.

The concern is not reuse itself.
The concern is releasing materials that are so close to the live paper that students can match question type to an almost identical full solution.

If the papers and written solutions are available, but the coded solutions are withheld until after the test, the assessment remains:

- supportive
- method-focused
- open book in a meaningful sense
- less vulnerable to rote pattern matching

## Practical Recommendation

Before the test, make available:

- lecture materials
- tutorial materials
- general example code from teaching
- mock and sample papers if desired
- practice papers
- written solutions to the practice papers

Before the test, keep hidden:

- coded solutions for the current practice papers
- any coded solution pack that mirrors the live paper structure too closely

After the test, release:

- full coded practice-paper solutions
- any additional worked verification code

## Suggested Short Version for Student-Facing Use

Students may use lecture slides, tutorial materials, their own notes, the released practice papers, and the released written solutions.
They may also use their own code/files.
They may not use AI tools.
Staff-prepared coded solutions for the current practice papers are not part of the permitted materials for the test and will be released after the assessment.
