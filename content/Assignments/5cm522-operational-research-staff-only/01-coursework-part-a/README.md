# Coursework Part A: staff materials

There is no code to run for Part A. Students get only the brief (public folder,
`01-coursework-part-a/assessment-brief-part-a.pdf`).

## Marking, step by step

1. Read the brief and **`part-a-model-solutions.docx`** (model answers to Questions 1–3: graphical LP,
   integer and binary models, solver output).
2. Make a working copy of **`part-a-marking-rubric-template.xlsx`** outside this folder. Duplicate the
   `Rubric` sheet once per student.
3. For each sub-question (rows Q1a–Q3d), enter a score out of 10 under the grade band you award. The
   `Mark` column converts it.
4. Score the three presentation rows (the brief's Appendix A rubric).
5. The total calculates automatically: answers are scaled to 85% and presentation to 15% (cell K35).
6. Enter final marks in the university's gradebook. Do not save completed sheets in this folder.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `part-a-model-solutions.docx` | Model answers to Questions 1–3 |
| `part-a-marking-rubric-template.xlsx` | Blank marking sheet: grade band per sub-question; Answers scaled to 85%, Presentation to 15% |
